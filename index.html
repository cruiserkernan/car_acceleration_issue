    How to use the graphs

    Select the vehicle signal you want to inspect from the complete OBD log.

    • The default comparison shows good Event 3 and bad Event 13. Choose “Show all runs” to see every event.
    • After selecting a signal in the desktop list, use the up and down arrow keys to step through signals.
    • Open “Series shown” below the graph to show or hide individual recorded runs.
    • Use “Show all at once” to see every signal stacked together.
